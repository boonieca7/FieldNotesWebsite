Back in Romania

Visa offices, a salt mine, Seat 41, the people of the semester, and finally Bran Castle.

7 field notes

10.05.2026Field Note Learning to Orient While Abroad Washing machines, signs, and insurance offices keep forcing me to stop and orient before acting. Judgment sometimes starts on a street corner, and the schnitzel I ordered turned out to be liver.
10.05.2026Field Note What a Visa Office Taught Me About AI Governance Three weeks of circular insurance rejections for a visa became a case study in opaque systems with no accountable owner. It draws on my research project "What Frameworks Cannot See."
27.05.2026Field Note Welcome Back to Romania A Turda weekend for the salt mine and a spa (the "Luca version" of the trip, after an imaginary Instagram travel bro) turned into an unplanned mountain hike, a broken hammam, and a lot of butterflies. A story worth having anyway.
27.05.2026Field Note The View From Seat 41 The trip home: a missed bus, an overpriced taxi, a sweltering city bus to Cluj, a surprise fancy hotel, and a late train. The plan failed, but the view of shepherds and their flocks did not.
06.06.2026Stories of Stories of Romania: Vignettes Short portraits of people from the semester: the librarian who was my first friend, a teacher, a taxi driver, coffee-shop regulars, and "Luca." I circled the word "earnestness" to come back to later.
06.06.2026Field Note Trebuie să Aștept Months of immigration-office visits with no status updates or progress bar. My Romanian test phrase finally fits: "I must wait" for a residence card that already exists somewhere in the building.
06.06.2026Field Note Which Castle Are You Visiting? Bran Castle turns out to be several castles at once: the Dracula souvenir economy, Queen Marie's home, the folklore, and Vlad's. A bookend to the Corvin Castle post, ending on hearts carved into doors.

Chrystin McLelland
