Arriving in Timișoara

Leaving home, landing in Romania, and learning the city one coffee, menu and Mărțișor charm at a time.

11 field notes

22.02.2026Field Note A Year in the Making: Saying Yes to Romania The backstory of applying to Erasmus a year earlier, while one part of my brain kept asking "who do I think I am?" It ends on the eve of departure, excited and uneasy about a season of not being the expert.
24.02.2026Field Note The Leaving, the Layover… The hardest part of leaving was my family, not the luggage, including a week of trying to turn "mom" into a checklist. A layover in Amsterdam dropped me into a Lunar New Year celebration that felt like permission to be delighted.
24.02.2026Field Note …and the Landing Arriving in Timișoara brought travel fog, the humility of the language barrier, and an airport that reminded me of Saipan. The dorm was a shock: not cozy, not home, and up several flights of stairs with 130 pounds of luggage.
24.02.2026Field Note Day One I turned panic into a shopping checklist and learned that "walkable" means something different in Romania. By the end the room felt livable and I felt capable, if not settled.
03.03.2026Field Note The Third Place A coffee shop by the dorm became my daily office, where 8 a.m. counts as early and nobody asks you to leave after seven hours. How the café treats time became a way to read how the culture treats space and pace.
03.03.2026Field Note Culture and Restaurants and Liver A practical primer on Romanian restaurant norms, starting with the fact that you seat yourself. A surprise plate of chicken liver pâté confirmed the universe's insistence that I eat liver.
03.03.2026Field Note It’s not you, it’s the culture American small talk with cashiers and drivers gets baffled looks, because friendliness with strangers can read as intrusion here. My accent gives me away instantly, and daily corrections from servers became my language practice.
03.03.2026Field Note The Coolest in the Parking Lot I assumed "DUB (ENG)" on a movie listing meant dubbed in English and sat through 100 minutes of the animated film GOAT in un-subtitled Romanian. I still cried at the ending, and decided humility is sometimes the price of a ticket.
14.03.2026Field Note Mărțișor Red-and-white charms appeared all over the city for March 1, and I learned the tradition behind them. The image I kept is an older man carefully pinning one to his partner's coat.
14.03.2026Stories of Stories of Romania: Baba Dochia Overlapping versions of the Baba Dochia and Dragobete legends tied to the arrival of spring. It closes with a list of Romanian idioms and their literal translations.
14.03.2026Field Note Women’s Day Flower stalls took over every corner around March 8 as people bought tulips for the women in their lives. Like Mărțișor, a month built from small gestures.
