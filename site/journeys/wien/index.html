Spring Break: Vienna

Traveling without a plan for once: white horses, a wrong turn on Easter, and three floors of the Belvedere.

8 field notes

08.04.2026Field Note Vienna: On getting it wrong For the first time I traveled without my usual color-coded Plan, arriving with one goal: see the white horses. Meet the planned version of me, who would have been efficient and missed things.
12.04.2026Field Note Vienna: Dancing White Horses and Chandeliers Watching a Lipizzaner training session at the Spanish Riding School in the Hofburg. The horses' visible mistakes and years of repetition handed me a lesson I give others and rarely apply to myself.
12.04.2026Field Note Vienna: A Kind of Pentimento Over coffee after the show, strangers told me how the Lipizzaners nearly disappeared in WWII. It connects to pentimento, the art term at the center of my leadership research.
13.04.2026Stories of Stories of Vienna: Once Upon a War A fairy-tale retelling of how the Third Reich gathered the Lipizzaners and Polish Arabians to breed a "master race" of horses, and how they were saved. It ends with Polish horsemen rebuilding their breed from a few dozen survivors.
13.04.2026Field Note Vienna: A Wrong Turn Hungry on Easter Sunday with no cash and a confused GPS, I ended up in a restaurant built on Roman ruins and had one of the best meals of my life. The planned version of me would have had a good trip and missed it.
16.04.2026Field Note Vienna: Making Faces Partway through the Belvedere I stopped looking at the art and started watching how people react to it. Then I went back to the entrance and walked the whole museum again.
16.04.2026Field Note Vienna: The Kiss and the Checkpoint The longest Vienna post: finally seeing Klimt's The Kiss in a crowd focused on getting the photo. It asks how museum design rewards documentation over encounter.
23.04.2026Field Note Vienna: What the Glass Takes On the Belvedere's quiet top floor, with less glass between me and the art, I thought about why famous paintings now sit behind protective glass after activist attacks. Something was lost, and I was lucky to see it before.
