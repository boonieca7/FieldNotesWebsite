Spring Break: Kraków

A cancelled tour that became a private one, and the city's dragon, bugler and enchanted pigeons.

3 field notes

25.04.2026Field Note Krakow: Unexpected Flying in instead of taking the train was a mistake, but the city surprised me. A trumpet call that keeps cutting off mid-note sets up a story for later.
25.04.2026Field Note Krakow: The Accidental Private Food Tour A group food tour cancels and becomes a one-on-one with guide Jason, a Portland transplant who came to help Ukrainian refugees and stayed. It ends with more vodka than planned and a classical concert next door.
25.04.2026Stories of Stories of Krakow: The Dragon, The Bugler, The Enchanted Knights Three Krakow legends: the Wawel dragon, the Saint Mary's bugler whose song breaks off, and knights turned into the pigeons of the market square. Told in the "bajka, nie bajka" (maybe true, maybe not) style.
