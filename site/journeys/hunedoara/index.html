Journeys / 02 · March 2026

Hunedoara and Corvin Castle

A castle, its legends and its stairs, and the small town that turned out to be more than its headline attraction.

12 field notes

09.03.2026Field Note Corvin Castle and the Problem with Dracula Corvin Castle, with its Gothic towers and moat, looks more like Dracula's castle than Bran does. It sets the castle up as a place where stories live and opens the Hunedoara cluster.
09.03.2026Stories of Stories of Romania: The Well of the Three Prisoners A retold legend of three Ottoman prisoners promised freedom by John Hunyadi if they dug a well through solid rock. Whether the story is true is left open, but someone did dig that well.
09.03.2026Field Note Climbing Corvin Castle A comic account of uneven medieval staircases and what they do to modern calves. The view from the top made it worth it.
09.03.2026Stories of Stories of Romania: The Raven of Corvin Castle A folktale about King Sigismund, a village woman, a ring, and a raven, explaining John Hunyadi's origins and his raven coat of arms. It ends where Hunyadi's own story begins.
09.03.2026Chronicles of Chronicles of Romania: John Hunyadi and Matthias Corvinus A short history of Hunyadi's wins against the Ottomans and his son Matthias's reign as a reforming, book-collecting king. My favorite fact is Hunyadi's contemporary nickname, "Turk-buster."
10.03.2026Chronicles of Chronicles of Romania: Vlad III of Wallachia A sourced look at Vlad the Impaler as Western horror icon, Romanian defender, and one brutal ruler in a brutal century. It asks why the same violence gets remembered so differently.
11.03.2026Field Note The Rhythm of a Small City A café-table sketch of Hunedoara in early spring, slower and quieter than Timișoara. Before tourist season the town still belongs to the people who live there.
13.03.2026Field Note Small Museum, Big Past A visit to Hunedoara's small Communism Museum, past factory gates 20,000 workers once crossed daily. Objects from the years I was a kid watching She-Ra made this history feel closer than the castle's.
14.03.2026Field Note Paper, Ink and Identity The museum's room of banknotes seemed out of place until I started reading money as a country's self-portrait. Mostly a captioned photo essay.
14.03.2026Field Note Tiny Worlds in Hunedoara A miniature train museum I found while video-calling Jason, who loves model trains. It turned into a reflection on hobbies as part of a life's story and a way to remember someone.
14.03.2026Field Note Following the Bells I followed church bells into an Orthodox service already underway and stood at the back trying not to intrude. A few minutes of chant and incense felt like stepping into another world.
14.03.2026Reflection Reflection: Hunedoara A wrap-up arguing the castle is only one layer of the town, alongside communist-era ruins, the promenade, and small museums. Staying put let the town become more than its headline attraction.
