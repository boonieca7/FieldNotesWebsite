București

An overnight train to the capital, a city shaped by communism and the 1977 earthquake, and one very long spa day.

5 field notes

21.03.2026Field Note Sleeper Train to Bucureşti My overnight train trip started with a misread ticket, a wandering Uber, and a sprint to the station. The train had been delayed all day, so the urgency was real but the problem wasn't.
05.04.2026Chronicles of Romania and Communism A history primer on how Romania came under communist rule and how ordinary people got by. It frames Romania as a country that absorbs change without disappearing.
05.04.2026Chronicles of Bucharest’s Empty Houses Why so many grand houses in "Little Paris" sit derelict, tracing it back to communist nationalization of private property. Through broken windows you can still see the fireplaces and crown moldings.
05.04.2026Chronicles of Bucharest: Crisis Creates Opportunities… But for Who? The 1977 earthquake gave the regime cover to bulldoze neighborhoods, which made me rethink a phrase I have leaned on for years in crisis work. Crisis doesn't decide what gets built or erased, people do.
07.04.2026Field Note Time for a spa day! Fourteen hours at a huge Bucharest thermal spa, saunas and red-light beds included. The highlight was a sauna ritual involving icy birch branches I did not knowingly sign up for.
